Why this book

Because the etiquette books never covered inflatable eagles

The newspaper only makes it worse. The neighbors have three new flags. Grandpa won’t take his cap off at church. This book is the calm, friendly voice your family needs, written in the trusted style of the old household handbooks, with a painting for every step.

One skill, one page

From spotting the first red cap to the handshake over the fence, every job fits on a single page.

Pictures for every step

Painted 1950s scenes of the family, the uncles and the neighbors in their red caps show exactly what to do.

Funny, never mean

Good-natured humor about the caps, the flags and the honking. Everyone is welcome at this table, and there is pie for all.

Made to print

US Letter pages with color tabs on the edge, ready for a binder or a print shop.

Anatomy of a skill page

Everything for the job on a single page

  1. Materials column

    A green side panel lists what to gather, from earmuffs to an emergency pie, with a picture of each item.

  2. Skill header

    The skill number, its chapter, how long it takes and how hard it is. "Skill 01 · The Morning It Began · 5 minutes · Easy."

  3. Numbered steps

    Six painted steps, each with a short title and plain instructions you can read across the Thanksgiving table.

  4. Notes and warnings

    The things that go wrong, called out before they happen. “Never say ‘Well, actually’ at Thanksgiving.”
