Because nobody else is going to tell you what to do

The pet shop has no aisle for it. The veterinarian has never seen one. The neighbors have questions. This book is the calm, friendly voice your family needs, written in the trusted style of the old household handbooks, with a painting for every step.

One skill, one page

Pictures for every step

Funny, never mean

Gentle family humor for all ages. Nobody gets eaten. The petunias are another matter.

Made to print

Anatomy of a skill page

Everything for the job on a single page

  1. Materials column

    A green side panel lists what to gather, from a garden hose to forty pounds of lettuce, with a picture of each item.

  2. Skill header

    The skill number, its chapter, how long it takes and how hard it is. "Skill 01 · The Discovery · 5 minutes · Easy."

  3. Numbered steps

    Six painted steps, each with a short title and plain instructions you can read across the backyard.

  4. Notes and warnings

    The things that go wrong, called out before they happen. “Never stand directly behind a stegosaurus.”
