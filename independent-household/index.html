Why this book

The know-how that kept a house running before the power grid

A healthy adult can last weeks without food but only about three days without water. Ice storms and heat waves can knock out power for a week. When the store shelves are empty or the doctor is an hour away, the household that knows a few old skills keeps going.

One skill, one page

Find it fast, follow it start to finish, and never flip back and forth mid-job.

Pictures for every step

Painted scenes show the hands-on work, so you can follow along even if you skim the words.

Cheap to do

Most skills use buckets, jars, salt and scrap wood you already have around the house.

Made to print

US Letter pages with color tabs on the edge, ready for a binder or a print shop.

Anatomy of a skill page

Everything for the job on a single page

  1. Materials column

    A green side panel lists what to gather, with a picture of each item so you know what to look for.

  2. Skill header

    The skill number, its chapter, how long it takes and how hard it is. "Skill 01 · Water · 1 weekend · Easy."

  3. Numbered steps

    Four to six painted steps, each with a short title and plain instructions you can read across the kitchen.

  4. Notes and warnings

    The things that go wrong, called out before they happen. "Never reuse milk jugs."
