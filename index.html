New on the shelf

Twenty-inch footprints led out of the pine woods and straight to the woodshed, and now Woodrow lives there: eight feet two, very hairy and with no manners whatsoever. Sixty wholesome skills for raising him right, from ducking through doorways and using a napkin to bath day with the garden hose and a disguise for church. Every skill fits on one painted page. 96 full-color pages, coming soon as a printed paperback.
