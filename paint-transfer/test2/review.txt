Common FLUX failure patterns:
1. Quoted instruction -> printed as a caption/speech bubble with misspellings (100%).
2. Fake signature and tiny printer's line in the bottom corners (~90%).
3. Book title rendered as a headline banner (~30%).
4. Occasional extra hand at the frame edge (armor f2, market f1, tea f2).
5. Action verbs get weakened: "wrapping the clock", "bowing", "catching in mid air", "climbs" often not shown.
6. Identical-looking duplicate characters (games boys), and costume confusion (vampire kids -> witch hats).
Suggested fix: drop the quoted instruction and book title from the prompt entirely, and describe only the visual scene.
